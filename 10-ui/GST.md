# GST UI

## Purpose
Prepare and file GST returns.

---

## Screens

1. **Period Dashboard** – summary of period (input tax, output tax, net)
2. **Input Tax Statement** – list of purchase invoices[reference:76]
3. **Output Tax Statement** – list of sales invoices[reference:77]
4. **Adjustments** – add credit/debit notes
5. **Review** – final review before export
6. **Export** – download MIRA 205/206[reference:78][reference:79]
