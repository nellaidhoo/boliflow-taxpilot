# E-Invoicing Integration

## Purpose
Support MIRA's electronic invoicing system.

---

## Background

MIRA is implementing an electronic invoicing system[reference:96]:
- Based on international models
- Direct connection between taxpayer platforms and MIRA systems
- Aims to simplify procedures and improve transparency

## Timeline[reference:97]

- Preliminary work began mid-2025
- Research into suitable models
- Expected rollout within 2026

## Requirements (Future)

- Receive e-invoices from MIRA
- Send e-invoices to MIRA
- Validate e-invoice format
- Auto-link e-invoices to tax periods
