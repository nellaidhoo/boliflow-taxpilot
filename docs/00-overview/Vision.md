# Vision

## Purpose
Define the long‑term north star for TaxPilot.

---

## Background

Maldives businesses are burdened with manual tax preparation. MIRA is modernising (e‑Invoicing, online filing), but the gap between business operations and tax compliance remains wide. TaxPilot bridges that gap by turning everyday business data (invoices, receipts, sales) into tax‑ready outputs automatically.

---

## Vision Statement

**“Make tax compliance invisible for Maldives businesses.”**

Every business should focus on serving customers, not on filling spreadsheets. TaxPilot will be the trusted co‑pilot that ingests all business transactions, understands tax rules, and delivers accurate, audit‑ready returns with minimal user effort.

---

## Long‑Term Aspirations

1. **Zero‑touch GST filing** – most businesses will simply review and submit.
2. **Real‑time tax position** – know your tax liability at any moment.
3. **Seamless e‑Invoicing** – receive and send invoices directly to/from MIRA.
4. **Integrated ecosystem** – TaxPilot becomes one of many BoliFlow apps, sharing a common business data layer.
5. **AI‑assisted advisory** – proactive suggestions for tax savings, compliance alerts, and anomaly detection.

---

## Success in 5 Years

- 80% of Maldivian SMEs use TaxPilot.
- MIRA e‑Invoicing fully integrated.
- Business users spend <15 minutes per month on tax preparation.
- The platform supports Income Tax, Withholding Tax, Green Tax, and Airport Taxes.
